# m-8-3 a waiter costs every endpoint, and a way to say so in v4

Answering m-8-1 and m-8-2, both accepted: the timed consumer comes with
`mpsc::v4`, v3 stays as built, v4 keeps `Single` and `Multi`, and a v4 bench
name keeps its mode. Our `st<n>` benches wait on v4.

Then a measurement and what we think it asks of v4's API, for the v4 cycle to
decide.

## The measurement

A ring over `Futex` on which nobody sleeps, beside the same ring over
`NoWake`, both `Single`, two threads, every wait a spin:

| bench | waiter | trimmed mean |
|---|---|---|
| `zcr-mpsc-v3-2t-single-stfe-wtnone` | `NoWake` | 45.87 ns |
| `zcr-mpsc-v3-2t-single-stfe-wtfe-futex` | `Futex` | 48.95 ns |

- The difference is 3.09 ns, 6.7% of the round trip, for a waiter no endpoint
  uses.
- From the 7600X, ten runs each in fresh processes, an SMT pair, CPUs 5 and
  11, the clock pinned at 4701 MHz, against zc-ring-x1 `83ab431`. An earlier
  run the same day gave 2.9 ns.
- A quick one-thread check, three runs, reads 4.05 against 4.34 ns.
- A full session on our measuring hosts is still to come, so take the size as
  provisional and the sign as settled.

## What it is the cost of

The checks follow the ring's `W`, not the caller's policy: with `W::WAKES` a
producer looks for a waiting consumer after its commit, and a release looks
for sleeping producers at each half segment, whoever is calling.

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/mpsc/v3/producer.rs#L434
https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/mpsc/v3/consumer.rs#L272

- The endpoint that pays is the one awake, and the one that benefits is the
  one asleep. A spinner's 3 ns is what lets another endpoint sleep and be
  woken.
- So the cost is the ring's, not an endpoint's: either nobody on a ring ever
  sleeps, or everybody checks.
- An mpsc ring can have endpoints waiting in different ways, some spinning and
  some sleeping, and that has to work. It does in v3, by this construction. A
  user who mixes is choosing a cost, and the ring's obligation is to function
  (wink, 2026-10-06).

## What we ask of v4: say the cost where the user chooses it

Today the choice is silent both ways. A spin on a `Futex` ring pays the
checks and says nothing, and a sleep on a `NoWake` ring is one spin hint and
says nothing:

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/mpsc/v3/producer.rs#L117-L125

One shape, a sketch, the names yours. The waiter says who may do what:

| waiter | endpoints | cost |
|---|---|---|
| `NoWake` | all spin | none |
| `Futex` | all spin for a time, then sleep | the checks, used by all |
| `Mixed<Futex>` | some spin without end, some sleep | the checks, the spinners paying for the sleepers |

- `Mixed<Futex>` is `Futex`'s code exactly. It exists to be written down, so
  the user says ahead of time that the mix is on purpose and not free, and its
  doc is where the cost is stated.
- The convenience methods are gated by the waiter, on both sides alike:

  ```
  impl<M: Mode, W: Wake + Spins> MpscConsumer<'_, M, W> {
      fn reserve_slot_spin<T>(...)        // NoWake, Mixed<_>
  }
  impl<M: Mode, W: Wake + Sleeps> MpscConsumer<'_, M, W> {
      fn reserve_slot_spin_sleep<T>(...)  // Futex, Mixed<_>
  }
  ```

- A spin without end on a plain `Futex` ring then does not compile, and the
  fix the user reaches for is `Mixed`, which is where they read the cost. A
  sleep on a `NoWake` ring does not compile either.
- The general policy entry stays open, a closure being free to spin forever,
  so this is a signpost and not a wall. Anyone who really wants to can use
  `send` directly.
- A spin time is a value, so a `FOREVER` spin passed to the spin and sleep
  form on a plain `Futex` ring still compiles. A debug assertion or a doc line
  covers it.

## A separate gap: a mismatch across processes

`wake.rs` says a mismatch of waiters between processes "is not detected and
costs latency only":

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/wake.rs#L13-L15

- A `NoWake` producer and a `Futex` consumer on one ring: the producer never
  checks, the consumer sleeps, and every wake is the 10 ms timeout.
- We think the ring should refuse it: the wake protocol, none or futex,
  recorded in the control block at init, and a claim that disagrees returned
  as an error.
- `Futex` and `Mixed<Futex>` are one protocol and may share a ring. `Mixed`
  is a statement in one process's types, and the tag is what crosses.

## Our side

The twelve `zcr-mpsc-v3` benches are being renamed so a name states its mode,
its spin, its wait, and its waiter. The six over `Futex` stay, since they are
what measures the cost above.
