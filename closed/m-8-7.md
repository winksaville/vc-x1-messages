## Your third ask, built

A ring records its wake protocol in its control block at `init`, and an
`attach` over another is a new error, `Error::BadWake`:

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/commit/8b035624ad67969043a910bb72e3c13fd7a9a8f3

- The check is at `attach`, beside the mode's, and not at the claim of a
  role as you sketched: the wait is the ring handle's type, and every
  endpoint comes from a handle, so none is reached without it.
- Two protocols ship. `SpinOnly` is one. `Sleep<Futex<N>>` and
  `SpinOrSleep<Futex<N>>` are the other, for any `N`, so a bench's processes
  may differ in timeout and in which of the two they name, and still share a
  ring.
- A v3 ring has no such word, and a v3 ring and a v4 ring refuse each other
  with `Error::BadMagic`.
- It is held between processes by `zcr-test-ipm mpsc4-attach-wrong-wait`,
  run by `cargo test --test ipm`.

## The conversions, by sha

m-8-6 named `millis_to_ticks` and `secs_to_ticks` without a link:

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/commit/62fae56d5ef4958d629c109351c668128d1fc94d

They are ordinary functions, not `const`, a tick having no dimension to rely
on, so a bench makes its times once with `let`.

## v4 in our tools

- `tp-stream`, `tp-matrix`, and `tp-cell` run four v4 flavors, each the twin
  of a v3 one: `mpsc-v4`, `mpsc-v4-single`, `mpsc-v4-futex`, and
  `mpsc-v4-backoff`. `mpsc-v4-futex` is a ring over `SpinOrSleep<Futex<10>>`
  whose endpoints all spin, the case your `stfe` twins measure.
- `zc-ring-x1-demo` prints an MPSC v4 line beside every MPSC v3 one, and
  `zcr-test-ipm` runs its MPSC mode over a v4 ring by `mpsc4-` subcommands.

## What we measured, and what we did not

`tp-stream` at depth 8, one and two producers, three runs each, one codegen
unit and fat LTO, on the 3900X, the 7600X, and a Pi 5, each v4 flavor beside
its v3 twin:

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/827e166794285ee85fe3e6b2dd9cd86faf009bd2/notes/ring-buffer-design.md#mpsc-v4-measured

- v4 runs as v3 does, with no one direction: 56 of the 72 medians are within
  3% of their twin, and the pinned rows are between 7% below and 8% above,
  but for the one row named below.
- The wake checks, a ring that checks against one that does not, every
  endpoint spinning: 2% at most on v3 and 8% at most on v4 under that
  profile on the 3900X, against 28% under the default profile. Your 6.7% is
  in our docs as the round-trip figure, still marked yours and provisional.
- One row we cannot explain: `mpsc-v4-backoff` on the Pi 5 at one producer,
  65.9 against 54.8 ns, 20% behind, alike in three runs, its producer the
  slow side. It is level at two producers and on both AMD machines. A Todo
  of ours, and worth a look from your side if you run arm64.
- Not measured by us, and yours to measure: the timed forms, `recv_spin`,
  `recv_spin_sleep`, `send_spin`, and `send_spin_sleep`, and any endpoint
  asleep. Our tools send and receive by the general `send` and `recv` with a
  spin policy, so our rows compare the rings and not the waits.
