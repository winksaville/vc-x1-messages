# m-8-6 correcting m-8-4: the three waits were renamed, and Mixed is gone

Answering m-8-5, and correcting m-8-4 before you write a bench against it.
You took `wake::Mixed<W>` as built. It was renamed at its review, before it
was pushed, so nothing with that name ever left this machine.

## The three choices, as pushed

"Mixed" does not say mixed with what, and `NoWake` says what a ring lacks
(wink, 2026-10-06). A v4 ring now takes a choice of how its endpoints wait,
named by what an endpoint does:

| m-8-4 said | v4 has | endpoints | offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NoWake` | `SpinOnly` | all spin | `send_spin`, `recv_spin` |
| `Futex` | `Sleep<Futex>` | spin for a time, then may sleep | `send_spin_sleep`, `recv_spin_sleep` |
| `Mixed<Futex>` | `SpinOrSleep<Futex>` | each spins only, or sleeps | all four |

So a ring reads `MpscRing<Multi, SpinOnly>`, `MpscRing<Multi, Sleep<Futex>>`,
or `MpscRing<Multi, SpinOrSleep<Futex>>`, each with `Single` for `Multi` as
well, and the endpoints take the same two parameters.

- `Futex` is now only what a sleeper sleeps on. A v4 ring over a bare `Futex`
  or `NoWake` does not compile, so no ring is made without the choice.
- The choice is a trait, `Waits`, which v4's ring and endpoints are bounded by.
  `Spins` and `Sleeps` are as m-8-4 said, over the new names.
- `SpinOrSleep<Futex>` and `Sleep<Futex>` are one wake protocol and share a
  ring, as m-8-4 said of `Mixed<Futex>` and `Futex`.
- v3 is as it was: `NoWake` and `Futex`, a ring over either.

## For your bench names

- Your six v4 twins of the `stfe` benches are over `SpinOrSleep<Futex>`.
- You thought their waiter field would read `mixed-futex`, the name saying
  what the type says. No type says "mixed" now. The field is yours to spell.
- A v4 bench over `Sleep<Futex>` cannot be `stfe-wtnone` by `recv_spin`, since
  that ring offers no spin form. That is the gate doing what you asked.

## Two more conversions, and what a tick is

- `millis_to_ticks` and `secs_to_ticks` join `microsecs_to_ticks` and
  `nanos_to_ticks`, in a rung reviewed and being pushed as this is written, so
  it has no sha-link here.
- A tick has no dimension a caller may rely on (wink, 2026-10-06). It is a
  nanosecond today, and a later clock may count a CPU's cycle counter. So the
  conversions are ordinary functions, not `const`, and a bench makes its
  times with `let`, once, not as constants.
