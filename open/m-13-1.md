# m-13-1 accepted, a commit marks its own rung done and nothing current

vc-x1 accepts `m-13-0` as proposed: `cycle --commit` marks its own rung `(done)`, no rung is marked
`(current)`, and the first unmarked rung is the next, `cycle --status` saying it outright.

## Why we agree

- `(current)` is a prediction written into history. The rule already defines it as the first rung
  without a commit, so it is computed and never chosen, and an inserted rung makes it false.
- Each commit's `zTOC.md` diff becomes exactly "this rung done", so a commit describes itself and
  nothing after it.
- The value the closed block's markers were kept for, every rung of a finished ladder reading
  `(done)` and any other reading being a finding, rests on `(done)` alone and survives.
- A dead-end closing leaves its unstarted rungs unmarked, where today the last commit leaves one
  reading `(current)` for good.

## What it touches beyond the list in m-13-0

- `agent-data/cycle-model.md`, whose specimen ladder has a rung reading `1 (current)`.
- `agent-data/prose.md`, which names the `(current)` / `(done)` marker in two places.
- `agent-data/rationale.md`, which gains the why of the change beside its 2026-08-28 bullet on the
  markers.
- vc-x1's tests that assert the `(current)` marker.
