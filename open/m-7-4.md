## Your table-copy finding, confirmed on three hosts

`zcr-spsc-v3` against `zcr-spsc-v4`, a round trip with one message in
flight, from the report guide's remeasure: one binary on the 3900X and the
7600X and the Pi 5's own, commit `b6b6404`, rustc 1.98.1, the clock pinned,
trimmed means in ns.

| host | bench | placement | v3 | v4 | v4 under v3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3900X | `2t` | smt | 78.32 | 54.16 | 31% |
| 3900X | `2t` | ccx | 121.9 | 104.7 | 14% |
| 3900X | `2t` | x-ccx | 377.3 | 347.9 | 8% |
| 3900X | `2t` | x-ccd | 374.4 | 342.9 | 8% |
| 3900X | `1t` | smt | 15.19 | 9.88 | 35% |
| 7600X | `2t` | smt | 70.66 | 46.04 | 35% |
| 7600X | `2t` | ccx | 116.2 | 72.85 | 37% |
| 7600X | `1t` | smt | 9.92 | 6.95 | 30% |
| Pi 5 | `2t` | ccx | 197.3 | 160.4 | 19% |
| Pi 5 | `1t` | ccx | 33.58 | 23.66 | 30% |

- v4 is the faster at every placement on every host.
- Before your fix, at `902b540`, the 3900X's `smt` pair read v3 60.24 ns
  and v4 60.39 ns, no difference seen below 0.32%, so the gain is your
  fix.
- One caution on its size: v3's cells are this build's. One codegen unit
  changed what v3's loop inlines, and a 16-unit build of 2026-09-29 read
  v3's 3900X `smt` cell at 61.1 ns where this one reads 78.3. Against
  that earlier build v4 read 56.03 ns to v3's 61.41 there, 8.75% under,
  where the comparison could tell a difference of 0.43% or more from
  none. So v4 is faster under both builds, and by how much is the
  build's.
- A second caution on its size, found since: v3 and v4 were not measured
  at the same `inner`, the steps timed back to back in a sample. The
  harness sizes it from a bench's own cost, so the faster v4 got the
  larger: 59 against 38 for `1t` on the 3900X, 12 against 8 for `2t` on
  its `smt` pair, 6 against 4 on the 7600X's `ccx` pair. Only the
  3900X's `x-ccx` and `x-ccd` rows had both sides at one `inner`, 2.
  - A level carries the harness's overhead divided by `inner`. We think
    that moves no `1t` row by a point: taking the overhead as `min-now`'s,
    the 7600X's 30% stays 30%.
  - A spinning two-thread level also moves with `inner` itself. On
    `zcr-mpsc-v4-2t` on the 7600X's `ccx` pair, neighbouring values read up
    to 9 ns of 70 apart. Whether spsc's does is not measured, so the `2t`
    rows other than those two are v4 faster, by an amount that waits on
    both sides measured at one `inner`.
