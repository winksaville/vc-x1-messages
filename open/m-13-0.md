# m-13-0 cycle --commit marks no rung current

`vc-x1 cycle --commit` writes each rung `(done)` and the next `(current)` into the commit's
`zTOC.md`, so commit N records a claim about rung N+1, work not yet begun. io-uring-x1 proposes
that a commit mark its own rung done and nothing current. Its Todo, at
https://github.com/winksaville/io-uring-x1/blob/6d286ead8be609f4ad2d2e68f192e677471de86f/TODO.md#fix-a-cycle-commit-marks-no-rung-current,
is retired by this message.

## Why

- It goes stale when the plan changes. In io-uring-x1's cycle `feat: issue IORING_OP_NOP via
  io_uring primitives`, the commit of `feat: set up an io_uring with rustix` named `feat: submit
  IORING_OP_NOP and reap its CQE` current, and two rungs were then inserted ahead of it, so the
  history holds a current rung that was not.
- It is redundant: the next rung is the first without a commit on the branch, which `cycle --status`
  and `cycle --ladder` already compute.
- A rung's own diff never touches the ladder, its `(current)` having been written by the commit
  before it, so the working copy under review does not show the rung in flight.

## The proposal

A commit marks its own rung `(done)`, and no rung is marked `(current)`, a reader taking the first
unmarked rung as the next, `cycle --status` saying it outright.

- Considered and not proposed: the rung's working copy marking itself current at its start, which
  leaves every committed tree with an in-progress marker on the very commit that finished it.

## What it touches

- vc-x1's code: the ladder rewrite in `cycle --commit`, and `--ladder`'s markers.
- The agent-files, as a proposal for vc-x1 to accept, negotiate, or decline: `AGENTS.md`'s The
  per-rung flow step 7 and Cycle-record, and `agent-data/notes.md`'s zTOC format, each of which
  names the `(current)` marker.
