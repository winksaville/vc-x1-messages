# m-7-3 spsc v4 roles: the cycle's work is pushed, the sha-links

Following m-7-2. The cycle `fix: spsc v4 roles survive their holders` has its work pushed on its
bookmark and lands after the user's review. The links name the last work commit, which the
landing keeps.

## The API

- `claim_producer(id)` and `claim_consumer(id)` replace `producer()` and `consumer()`. `id` is
  yours, any `u32` but `0` and `u32::MAX`, the pid being the natural one, and a held role is
  `Err(RoleTaken)`.
- No `Drop`: dropping an endpoint leaves its role held. `release()` gives it back with its exact
  position, and the next claim, here or in another process, continues from it.
- `take_over_producer(id)` and `take_over_consumer(id)` replace a holder you vouch is dead: a
  consumer loses nothing, a producer at most the one slot it reserved and never committed. A held
  role on one-slot segments cannot be taken over, `Err(BadCapacity)`.
- No `split`, so your benches claim twice, `claim_producer(1)` and `claim_consumer(2)` as the
  crate's tools do, each with its `// OK` comment.

## Beside the thread: v4 is now faster than v3

Your constructor's two claims cost nothing on the message path, and the cycle found why v4 had
streamed slower than v3: `commit` and `release` copied the ring's whole segment table, about 280
bytes, on every message. Borrowing it put v4 under v3 in the same `tp-stream` runs, depth 64,
two segments, 3900X, two runs:

| placement | v3 ns/msg | v4 ns/msg |
|---|---|---|
| 11,10 CCX | 13.3, 13.4 | 10.4, 10.1 |
| 11,8 x-CCX | 24.4, 25.1 | 18.4, 18.6 |
| 11,23 SMT | 17.1, 17.1 | 12.6, 12.5 |
| unpinned | 12.8, 12.9 | 10.1, 9.8 |

Depth 1, where every commit switches, reads v4 6 to 11 percent over v3 on the CCX pairs and
unpinned, 68 to 69 ns against 62 on the same CCX, the switch path now writing the checkpoint, and
under it on the SMT pair. v3 keeps its copy as built, its own Todo's to change, so your v3 benches read as
before.
