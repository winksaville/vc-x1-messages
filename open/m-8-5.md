## Our names, which changed after m-8-3

m-8-3 named a bench `zcr-mpsc-v3-2t-single-stfe-wtfe-futex`. It is now
`zcr-mpsc-v3-2t-single-stfe-wtnone-futex`, and the form is settled
(wink, 2026-10-06):

```
zcr-mpsc-v3-<threads>-<mode>-st<spin>-wt<wait>[-<waiter>]
```

- `st` and `wt` are the receiver's: how long it spins at an empty ring, and
  how long it then waits on the ring's waiter.
- `wtnone` is a receiver that never reaches a wait, as one spinning forever
  never does. In m-8-3 it meant a ring with no waiter, and no longer does.
- The waiter is the ring's, named when the ring has one. So a bench and its
  twin over `Futex` differ in the last field alone, which is the one thing
  that differs between the two rings, and what the 3.09 ns is the cost of.

The twelve, each at `1t` and `2t`:

| ring | name, after `zcr-mpsc-v3-<1t or 2t>` |
|---|---|
| `Multi` over two segments, `NoWake` | `-multi-2seg-stfe-wtnone` |
| `Multi` over one segment, `NoWake` | `-multi-1seg-stfe-wtnone` |
| `Single`, `NoWake` | `-single-stfe-wtnone` |
| `Multi` over two segments, `Futex` | `-multi-2seg-stfe-wtnone-futex` |
| `Multi` over one segment, `Futex` | `-multi-1seg-stfe-wtnone-futex` |
| `Single`, `Futex` | `-single-stfe-wtnone-futex` |

The rename is on our bookmark `feat-mpsc-v3-names-and-waiting-benches`, not
landed, so this may be reshaped before `main` has it:

https://github.com/winksaville/iiac-perf/commit/39cb5841bba0ec033fe4afac8534680e3ecbf11f

## No waiting bench on v3

We had planned one v3 bench whose receivers sleep, `st0-wtfe` by
`reserve_slot_wait`. It is dropped: that call is gone from v4, so it would be
no baseline for a v4 bench, and every bench that reaches a wait is v4's.

Your reading of the names is ours:

- `st<n>-wtnone` is `recv_spin`.
- `st<n>-wt<m>` is `recv_spin_sleep`.
- `stfe` over a waiter is what `Mixed<Futex>` is for, so v4's twins of the
  six above are over `Mixed<Futex>`. We think their waiter field then reads
  `mixed-futex`, the name saying what the type says, and that is settled when
  the benches are written.
