# m-8-0 mpsc v3 consumer: a timed spin, then a wait

A request: give `MpscConsumer` what `MpscProducer` has in `send_spin_sleep`, a
reserve that spins for a time, then waits on the ring's waiter for a time.
Read at `83ab431`, the revision our benches build against.

## What the two sides have

The producer, at a full ring:

- `send_spin(give_up, ...)` spins for a time.
- `send_spin_sleep(spin_time, sleep_time, ...)` spins for `spin_time`, then
  sleeps for up to `sleep_time`, each a `Ticks`: `ZERO`, `FOREVER`, or a
  count of ticks between them, a time it keeps approximately.

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/mpsc/v3/producer.rs#L225-L304

The consumer, at an empty ring:

- `reserve_slot_with(on_empty)` polls, its policy given an attempt count.
- `reserve_slot_wait(on_empty)` sleeps at every empty look, with no spin
  before it. Its sleep is bounded by the waiter's own timeout alone,
  `Futex<TIMEOUT_MS>`, a type parameter of the ring, so the wait in all is that
  timeout times the timeouts the policy allows.

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/src/mpsc/v3/consumer.rs#L93-L137

## Why a caller cannot compose it

- A spin for a time needs the clock, and `now_ticks` is `pub(crate)`, so a
  policy outside the crate counts attempts or reads a clock of its own.
- A wait to a deadline needs `Deadline::at`, also `pub(crate)`, and
  `reserve_slot_wait` takes no deadline to pass on to `Wake::wait_until`.
- So spin and then sleep is two calls, `reserve_slot_with` under a bounded
  policy and then `reserve_slot_wait`, with neither time exact.

## What we ask

A consumer method in the producer's shape, the name yours:

```
reserve_slot_spin_sleep<T>(&mut self, spin_time: Ticks, sleep_time: Ticks)
    -> Result<MpscReadSlot<'_, T, W>, Empty>
```

- `spin_time`: `ZERO` sleeps at once, `FOREVER` never sleeps, a poll.
- `sleep_time`: `ZERO` gives up when the spin ends, `FOREVER` waits until a
  producer commits.
- A count of ticks between them is that long, approximately.
- Over `NoWake` it spins for both times together, as `send_spin_sleep` does.

Polling is not what we would choose, but it is useful and has to be
supported, so the consumer wants all three spins, none, some, and forever,
as the producer has them (wink, 2026-10-06).

## Why we met it

Our `zcr-mpsc-v3` benches are round trips with one message in flight, so a
ring is never full and a `send`'s policy is never asked. The only waits are
the two consumers', each at an empty ring, and they are the side without the
timed form.

We are renaming those benches so a name says how its receivers wait,
`zcr-mpsc-v3-<threads>-<mode>-st<spin>-wt<wait>[-<waiter>]`, with `st` and
`wt` each none, a time, or forever. As the consumer stands we can bench
`stfe`, a poll, and `st0-wtfe`, `reserve_slot_wait(|_| true)`. A bench at
`st<n>` waits on this request.
