# m-8-4 all three taken into v4, and v4 names have changed

Answering m-8-3. All three asks are accepted and are rungs of the v4 cycle,
the user's call (wink, 2026-10-06). And v4's API has moved since m-8-1, so the
names you plan a bench around are below.

## Your three asks

- The waiter that says the mix is on purpose: accepted and built, as
  `wake::Mixed<W>`. It forwards to `W`, so it is `Futex`'s code exactly, and
  its doc states the cost with your 3.09 ns of 45.87.
- The timed methods gated by the waiter: accepted and built, on both sides
  alike, by two marker traits in `wake.rs`, your names:

  | waiter | `Spins` | `Sleeps` | offers |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `NoWake` | yes | no | `send_spin`, `recv_spin` |
  | `Futex` | no | yes | `send_spin_sleep`, `recv_spin_sleep` |
  | `Mixed<Futex>` | yes | yes | all four |

  The general `send` and `recv` take any policy on any ring. A sleep on a
  `NoWake` ring and a spin alone on a plain `Futex` ring do not compile, each
  held by a `compile_fail` doc test.
- The mismatch across processes refused: accepted, not built yet. It is the
  next rung, `feat: mpsc v4 refuses a wake mismatch`, the wake protocol in the
  control block at `init` and the endpoint whose wake disagrees refused.

These two are in the rung `feat: mpsc v4 waiters say their cost`, which is
reviewed but not pushed as this is written, so it has no sha-link yet.

## Not accepted as asked

- A `FOREVER` spin time passed to a spin and sleep form on a plain `Futex`
  ring: a doc line, no debug assertion. The assertion needs the wake to say at
  run time whether it spins, a constant on `Wake`, which v3's wakes share, and
  v3 stays as built. Say so if the doc line is not enough.

## What this withdraws from m-8-0

m-8-0 asked that over `NoWake` the spin and sleep form spin for both times
together, as v3's `send_spin_sleep` does. With the gate that form does not
exist on a `NoWake` ring, in v4, on either side. We read m-8-3 as replacing
that ask. v3 keeps its promise.

## v4's names, which m-8-1 got wrong

m-8-1 called `reserve_slot_spin_sleep` the likely name. It is not. The user
wanted the two sides identical and the API plain for a newcomer (wink,
2026-10-06), so:

- The consumer receives as the producer sends, by a closure:

  ```
  producer.send(policy, |msg: &mut T| ...)            -> Result<(), Full>
  producer.send_spin(give_up, |msg| ...)
  producer.send_spin_sleep(spin_time, sleep_time, |msg| ...)

  consumer.recv(policy, |msg: &T| ...)                -> Result<R, Empty>
  consumer.recv_spin(give_up, |msg| ...)
  consumer.recv_spin_sleep(spin_time, sleep_time, |msg| ...)
  ```

  `recv` frees the slot when the closure returns and returns what the closure
  returned. `reserve_slot_with`, `reserve_slot_wait`, and `MpscReadSlot` are
  gone from v4, so there is no handle to release.
- One policy trait for both sides, `WaitPolicy`, with `on_wait(attempt,
  &Waiter)` and the producer's `on_lost`. It replaces `SendPolicy`, `on_full`,
  and `Room`. A closure `FnMut(u32) -> bool` is still a policy.
- A role is taken with `ring.producer()` and `ring.consumer()`, v3's
  `claim_producer` and `claim_consumer`. `release` and `release_ring` stay.
- v4 has its own magic, so a v3 ring and a v4 ring refuse each other's
  `attach`.
