# m-8-1 the timed consumer comes with mpsc v4, not v3

Accepted, and it comes with `mpsc::v4`. v3 stays as built, so your benches at
`83ab431` keep building and no v3 method changes or appears.

## Where it lands

v4 is the Todo that starts as a copy of v3 with the `Single` and `Multi` mode
dropped:

https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/83ab4318154260233ba797d3565d87dd472a56b8/TODO.md#mpsc-v4-v3-without-single-and-multi

The consumer's waits join it. The cycle is not open yet, so there is no date,
and a bench at `st<n>` waits on v4.

## What you found, confirmed

- `MpscConsumer` already carries the ring's `W: Wake`, and `reserve_slot_wait`
  sleeps through `W::wait`, so no new waiter is needed.
- `sleep_empty` never calls `W::wait_until`, so the consumer's sleep has no
  deadline, and the shared reserve sleeps at every empty look or at none.
- `now_ticks` and `Deadline::at` are `pub(crate)`, so a caller cannot compose
  it, as you say.

## The shape, the user's lean

The two sides are to be symmetric, and identical where the semantics are the
same (wink, 2026-10-06):

- Same semantics: the same names, parameters, and meanings on both sides, so
  the consumer gets all three spins, none, some, and forever, as the producer
  has them.
- Semantics that differ: we first understand why, and what would have to
  change to make them the same.
- Where they cannot be made the same: the names differ, so a name never
  promises a likeness that is not there.

So the name you proposed, `reserve_slot_spin_sleep`, and its `spin_time` and
`sleep_time` are the likely form, but the names are settled in the v4 cycle,
not here. We think the consumer also gets a general policy entry, as `send` is
to `send_spin` and `send_spin_sleep`, with v3's `reserve_slot_with` and
`reserve_slot_wait` becoming policies over it.

- The names you can bench today, `stfe` and `st0-wtfe`, are v3's, and the
