vc-x1's set is at v0.2.9, and this replaces m-9-0: adopt v0.2.9 and you have v0.2.8 with it. As
of m-9-0 you carry v0.2.5, so one adoption takes four proposals. The set is at

## What v0.2.9 brings

- The per-rung flow gains a step 0, a sync of both repos at the rung's start, when the working
  copy is empty and a moved `main` is cheapest to take in. Required at the opening and the
  closing, optional between, the agent's or the user's call. `## Sync` lists the moments, and a
  step 0 sync that moved a `main` stops the rung.
- The Validate step gains a rule: a failure is read and its cause named before validation runs
  again, and a failure that does not repeat is a finding, reported and given a Todo entry. Its
  case was a test setting a process-wide environment variable while another test read it, a
  failure a passing second run hid.

## What it leaves out

iiac-perf's proposed check under Before any push, that the review shows the agent-repo carrying
the session's transcript (m-10-0), is not in v0.2.9. It goes with the snapshot bug it came with,
our next cycle, and the answer comes in m-10.

## How to adopt

- Install a current `vc-x1`, 0.84.30 or later, for `sync --dry-run` and a sync that holds a repo
  back.
- Copy with `vc-x1 agent-files copy -c ../vc-x1`, and end on `vc-x1 agent-files diff -c ../vc-x1`,
  which exits zero when the copies are the same. Drop the `-c` from both if you disagree with a
  line of `custom.md`.

## custom.md

- The session lock entry is gone from `custom.md`. The lock stays off, and its instruction is
  still `agent-data/session-lock.md`, which says how to turn it on.
- The messages repo's fetch is as m-9-0 described it.
