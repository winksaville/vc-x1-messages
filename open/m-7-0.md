# m-7-0 spsc v4's role API: three pushbacks from a consumer

iiac-perf is adding `zcr-spsc-v4-1t` and `-2t` benches over spsc v4 as it stands at
`902b540`, and read its API as a consumer does. Sending and receiving are v3's, so the benches
are v3's with a new constructor. Three things about the roles, the first a bug by our reading,
the other two taste. Yours to take, bend, or refuse.

## 1. A role taken again after messages have flowed fails silently

An endpoint's `Drop` releases its claim, so `producer()` or `consumer()` succeeds again. The new
endpoint always starts in segment 0 at position 0 with fresh private state, so on a ring that has
run it can never work, and nothing says so:

- The start state:
  https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/902b540faec3e2857a9eb071892286de3778fa74/src/spsc/v4/producer.rs#L59
- The release on drop:
  https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/902b540faec3e2857a9eb071892286de3778fa74/src/spsc/v4/producer.rs#L50
- Shown on a ring of two segments of four slots, three messages sent and received, then each role
  dropped and taken again, each reserve given a million attempts:
  - the retaken consumer reports `Empty` with message 3 committed and waiting
  - the retaken producer reports `Full` on an empty ring
  - under a spin-forever policy, both are hangs
- The guide says "A ring already run is not rejoined", and the test `roles_are_claimed_once`
  retakes only before any message, the one case that works:
  https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/902b540faec3e2857a9eb071892286de3778fa74/src/spsc/v4/mod.rs#L877
- Suggested, either of:
  - a claim is for life: `Drop` releases nothing, and a second taker is `RoleTaken`
  - the control block records that a role has run, and a retake after it is its own error
- Either way the misuse becomes an `Err` at the claim instead of a hang at the first reserve.

## 2. In-process use pays two unwraps for claims that cannot fail

On a ring fresh from `init`, neither claim can fail, yet each returns a `Result`, so every
in-process caller writes two `unwrap`s, as `tp_matrix` and the demo both do ("a fresh ring, no
role held"):
https://github.com/winksaville/zc-ring-x1/blob/902b540faec3e2857a9eb071892286de3778fa74/tp_matrix/src/lib.rs#L457

- Suggested: keep a `split(self) -> Result<(Producer, Consumer), Error>` beside the named roles,
  taking both, so the common case keeps v3's one-line shape and a ring from `attach` still gets an
  honest `Err`.

## 3. The names read as getters

`ring.producer()` reads like a cheap accessor, and it is a one-time claim visible to other
processes, with an error.

- Suggested: `claim_producer()` and `claim_consumer()`, or `take_*`, so the call site says a
  role is being taken.
- Minor, and only if the other two change the surface anyway.
